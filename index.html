    Data sources and how it works

    The 120th Congress’s terms begin at noon Eastern on January 3, 2027. Twentieth Amendment

    Esri matches your address, and Census TIGERweb assigns House districts for each Congress. Missouri used its 119th Congress map for the 2026 general election. Census TIGERweb · 2026 district notes

    Office contacts come from the House Clerk, the U.S. Senate and the Congress Legislators project. House directory · Senate directory · Congress Legislators

    Election calls come from civicAPI and may precede official certification. Campaign links come from Votewiser or candidates’ websites. civicAPI · Votewiser
